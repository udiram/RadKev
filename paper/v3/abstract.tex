\noindent\textbf{Background:} Many radiology decisions, such as whether a report describes a finding or how urgently a study should be read, are choices among a fixed set of answers. Decision models return a probability for each permitted answer in one step, without generating text, and an open-weight model can run within a hospital so that patient data stay local. No decision model has yet been specialized for radiology and compared with large language models (LLMs).

\noindent\textbf{Methods:} We fine-tuned the general-purpose decision models Kev-27B (Qwen3.8-27B backbone) and Kev-9B on \V{v3_train_records} records from public radiology datasets to obtain RadKev-27B and RadKev-9B, and compared them with the Kev models, the hosted decision models Jev and OpenAI Decisions, and the LLM Qwen3.8-27B, on \V{v3_bench_q} held-out questions in \V{r3_ntask} tasks (report reading, case diagnosis and classification, radiological knowledge). The prespecified primary outcome was task-averaged accuracy relative to Kev-27B.

\noindent\textbf{Results:} RadKev-27B exceeded Kev-27B by \V{r3_prim_tm} percentage points (95\% CI \V{r3_prim_tm_ci}), with the largest gain in report error detection (\V{r3_prim_t_rexerr} points); RadKev-9B exceeded Kev-9B by \V{r3_spec9_tm} points (\V{r3_spec9_tm_ci}) and did not differ detectably from Kev-27B, a model three times its size. RadKev-27B was more accurate than the hosted decision models Jev (\V{r3_jv_tm} points, \V{r3_jv_tm_ci}) and OpenAI Decisions (\V{r3_od_tm}, \V{r3_od_tm_ci}), than Qwen3.8-27B without reasoning (\V{r3_q_tm}, \V{r3_q_tm_ci}) and with reasoning (\V{r3_rp_rk_qthink_tm} points, \V{r3_rp_rk_qthink_tm_ci}) on \V{r3_rs_q} questions, at \V{r3_lt_ratio_think}-fold lower latency (\V{r3_lt_rk27} ms vs \V{r3_lt_qthink} s per question). At an error rate of 5\%, RadKev-27B could answer \V{r3_bcov_v3_27}\% of questions and Kev-27B \V{r3_bcov_stock27}\%. % [C1] [C4] [C9] [C19] [C20] [C23] [C26]

\noindent\textbf{Conclusions:} RadKev-27B was more accurate than two hosted decision models and an LLM of the same size, with or without reasoning, at a fraction of the latency of reasoning, and can be deployed within an institution for categorical radiology decisions after validation on local data. % [C4] [C19] [C23] [C26]
