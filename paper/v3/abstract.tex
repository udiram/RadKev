\noindent\textbf{Background:} System One-like decision models are built to return probabilities over a pre-populated answer space to specific questions in a single forward pass, a format well suited to radiology decisions, and an open-weight decision model can be deployed within an institution to assist with decision-making workflows. However, no decision model has been specialized for radiology and compared with large language models (LLMs).

\noindent\textbf{Methods:} Here, we fine-tuned the general-purpose decision models Kev-27B (Qwen3.8-27B backbone) and Kev-9B on \V{v3_train_records} records from open sourced radiology datasets to obtain RadKev-27B and RadKev-9B, and compared them with the Kev models and two LLMs of the same size, Qwen3.8-27B and MedGemma-27B-text, on \V{v3_bench_q} held-out questions in \V{r3_ntask} tasks (report reading, case diagnosis and classification, radiological knowledge). The prespecified primary benchmark was task-averaged accuracy relative to Kev-27B.

\noindent\textbf{Results:} RadKev-27B exceeded Kev-27B by \V{r3_prim_tm} percentage points (95\% CI \V{r3_prim_tm_ci}), with the largest gains in report error detection (\V{r3_prim_t_rexerr} points) and Eurorad diagnosis (\V{r3_prim_t_eurodx} points); RadKev-9B exceeded Kev-9B by \V{r3_spec9_tm} points (\V{r3_spec9_tm_ci}) and did not differ detectably from Kev-27B, a model three times its size. RadKev-27B was more accurate than Qwen3.8-27B (\V{r3_q_tm} points, \V{r3_q_tm_ci}) and MedGemma-27B-text (\V{r3_mg_tm}, \V{r3_mg_tm_ci}) without reasoning, and did not differ detectably from Qwen3.8-27B with reasoning (\V{r3_rp_rk_qthink_tm} points, \V{r3_rp_rk_qthink_tm_ci}) on \V{r3_rs_q} questions, at \V{r3_lt_ratio_think}-fold lower latency (\V{r3_lt_rk27} ms vs \V{r3_lt_qthink} s per question). At 5\% error, RadKev-27B answered \V{r3_bcov_v3_27}\% of questions and Kev-27B \V{r3_bcov_stock27}\%. % [C1] [C4] [C5] [C9] [C19] [C20]

\noindent\textbf{Conclusions:} RadKev-27B was more accurate than LLMs of the same size and did not differ detectably from one that reasons, at a fraction of its latency, and can be deployed within an institution in their place for categorical radiology decisions. % [C4] [C5] [C19]
