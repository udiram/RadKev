# Figures

The nine figures of the manuscript, as PNG and PDF, numbered as in the manuscript. They are generated by
[`paper/figures.py`](../paper/figures.py) from the aggregate results; the captions are in [`paper/main.tex`](../paper/main.tex).

| File | Manuscript | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| `fig1_teacher` | Figure 1 (Methods) | two-teacher labeling: one retained and one discarded order question |
| `fig2_kev` | Figure 2 (Methods) | architecture of Kev, the decision model fine-tuned in this study |
| `fig3_training` | Figure 3 (Methods) | training loss of RadKev-27B and RadKev-9B and of the two 9B initializations; development accuracy by source |
| `fig4_primary` | Figure 4 (Results) | primary comparison: Kev-27B and RadKev-27B by task average and decision family |
| `fig5_llm` | Figure 5 (Results) | decision models and LLMs: human-labeled accuracy, reasoning sample, latency |
| `fig6_spec` | Figure 6 (Results) | specialization, scale and initialization |
| `fig7_calib` | Figure 7 (Results) | calibration and selective prediction on human-labeled questions |
| `fig8_robust` | Figure 8 (Results) | robustness: options-only control, alternative answer options, distinctive words of the correct option in the case |
| `fig9_answer_space` | Figure 9 (Results) | accuracy and latency as the answer space grows |

Figure 1 reproduces text from Eurorad cases 17094 and 18086, © European Society of Radiology, licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; that
