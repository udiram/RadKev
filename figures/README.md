# Figures

The nine figures of the manuscript, as PNG and PDF. They are generated by [`paper/figures.py`](../paper/figures.py) (Figures 1 to 3)
and [`paper/v3/build_v3.py`](../paper/v3/build_v3.py) (Figures 4 to 9) from the aggregate results in `paper/inputs/`; the captions
are in [`paper/main.tex`](../paper/main.tex) and [`paper/v3/results.tex`](../paper/v3/results.tex).

| File | Manuscript | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `fig1_teacher_labels` | Figure 1 (Methods) | two-teacher labeling: one retained and one discarded order question |
| `fig2_architecture` | Figure 2 (Methods) | architecture of Kev, the decision model fine-tuned in this study |
| `fig3_training` | Figure 3 (Methods) | training loss, training accuracy and time per optimizer step of RadKev-27B and RadKev-9B; development accuracy by source |
| `fig4_primary` | Figure 4 (Results) | Kev-27B and RadKev-27B on the radiology benchmark: task means and every task |
| `fig5_comparison` | Figure 5 (Results) | decision models and LLMs: accuracy of every system, RadKev-27B and Qwen3.8-27B with and without reasoning, latency |
| `fig6_specialization` | Figure 6 (Results) | specialization and scale; general decision performance on Kev's transfer suite |
| `fig7_calibration` | Figure 7 (Results) | calibration and selective prediction on the human-labeled questions |
| `fig8_robustness` | Figure 8 (Results) | subspecialty classification before the read, instruction wording, options-only control |
| `fig9_answer_space` | Figure 9 (Results) | accuracy and latency as the answer space grows |

Colors: RadKev in blue, the other decision models in grey, the LLMs in tan.

Figure 1 reproduces text from Eurorad cases 17094 and 18086, © European Society of Radiology, licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; that
figure is shared under the same license.
